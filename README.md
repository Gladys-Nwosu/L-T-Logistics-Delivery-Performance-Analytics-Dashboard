# 🚚 L&T Logistics Delivery Performance Analytics Dashboard 
## TS Academy Capstone Project
## Author: Gladys C Nwosu
---
## Project Overview

This project analyzes L&T logistics operations to understand delivery performance, fleet efficiency, cost drivers, and profitability.

It helps identify where delays happen, what causes them, and how the business can improve delivery speed and reduce operational cost.

The dashboard was built using Power BI.

---

## Business Problem

The main goal of this project was to understand:

- Where are deliveries most delayed?
- Which routes and cities are underperforming?
- What is affecting on-time delivery performance?
- How is the fleet performing in terms of fuel usage and efficiency?
- Which trucks have the highest maintenance costs?
- How do customers contribute to revenue and profit?
- Is profitability improving over time?
- Which areas of the business need attention?

---

## Key Insights

- Nearly half of all deliveries are delayed.
- A few routes contribute most of the delivery issues.
- Indianapolis and some key routes show the highest delays.
- Fuel is the biggest cost driver (about 92% of total cost).
- Fleet fuel efficiency is low (6.5 MPG).
- TRK00003 has significantly higher maintenance cost at over $90K than others.
- Profitability has improved over time (60% → 65%) between 2022 and 2024.
- Contract customers generate the highest profit at $71M.
- Seattle and Portland are the most profitable cities, outperforming larger markets like Chicago.
- A small number of drivers account for most incidents.

---

## Key Recommendations

- Focus on fixing high-delay routes first.
- Improve fuel efficiency to reduce operating costs.
- Replace TRK00003 and review other high-maintenance trucks.
- Increase conversion from Spot to Contract customers.
- Expand operations in high-performing cities.
- Provide coaching for drivers with high incident rates.
